\# Phase 4 – Customer Sales Analytics Mini Project



\## Overview



Phase 4 is a mini project that focuses on analyzing customer sales data using SQL and PySpark. The project demonstrates an end-to-end data processing pipeline, including data loading, cleaning, transformation, business analytics, customer segmentation, report generation, and exporting the final report.



This phase integrates SQL queries with PySpark DataFrame operations to solve real-world business problems and generate meaningful insights from customer and sales datasets.



\---



\## Objectives



\- Load customer and sales datasets.

\- Clean and validate the datasets.

\- Remove duplicate and invalid records.

\- Perform business analytics.

\- Generate customer reports.

\- Segment customers based on spending.

\- Export the final report.



\---



\## Project Workflow



\### Step 1 – Data Loading



\- Create Spark Session

\- Read Customer Dataset

\- Read Sales Dataset

\- Display Data

\- Print Schema



\---



\### Step 2 – Data Cleaning



The following data cleaning operations are performed:



\- Remove NULL values

\- Remove Duplicate Records

\- Filter Invalid Customer IDs

\- Filter Invalid Sales Records

\- Display Cleaned Data

\- Compare Original and Clean Record Counts



\---



\### Step 3 – Business Analytics Tasks



\#### Task 1 – Daily Sales



Calculates the total sales generated for each day.



\*\*Output\*\*



\- Sale Date

\- Total Sales



\---



\#### Task 2 – City-wise Revenue



Calculates total revenue generated by each city.



\*\*Output\*\*



\- City

\- Total Revenue



\---



\#### Task 3 – Top 5 Customers



Displays the top five customers based on total spending.



\*\*Output\*\*



\- Customer ID

\- Customer Name

\- Total Spend



\---



\#### Task 4 – Repeat Customers



Identifies customers who placed more than one order.



\*\*Output\*\*



\- Customer ID

\- Order Count



\---



\#### Task 5 – Customer Segmentation



Customers are classified into three categories based on their total spending.



\### Segments



\- Gold

\- Silver

\- Bronze



\---



\#### Task 6 – Final Reporting Table



Generates a complete customer analytics report containing:



\- Customer ID

\- Customer Name

\- City

\- Total Spend

\- Order Count

\- Customer Segment



\---



\#### Task 7 – Export Final Report



The final reporting table is exported as a CSV file and loaded again to verify the output.
