\# Phase 4 - Business Analytics and Reporting



\## Overview



Phase 4 focuses on analyzing customer sales data using SQL and PySpark. Various business reports are generated to understand customer behavior, sales performance, and revenue trends. This phase demonstrates how analytical queries can support business decision-making.



\## Objectives



\- Perform business analytics on sales data.

\- Generate customer reports.

\- Analyze revenue patterns.

\- Build analytical reporting tables.



\## Topics Covered



\### Business Analytics

\- Daily Sales Analysis

\- City-wise Revenue Analysis

\- Customer Spending Analysis

\- Repeat Customer Identification

\- Customer Segmentation

\- Final Reporting



\### SQL

\- GROUP BY

\- Aggregate Functions

\- CASE Statements

\- INNER JOIN

\- Reporting Queries



\### PySpark

\- groupBy()

\- agg()

\- join()

\- sum()

\- count()

\- when()

\- orderBy()
